History — archive browser

Cross-event archive over GET /v1/history?place=&layer=&from=&to= — every stored revision of every event in the time range, newest first: the after-action review view. Filters map 1:1 to the endpoint's query parameters and live in this page's URL, so a date-range view of an incident's whole arc is a shareable permalink. Pagination follows next_page_token. Timestamps are RFC 3339; the response is the snake_case protojson of grid.v1.EventRevisionList (revisions[].{revision, observed_at, ingested_at, event}).
