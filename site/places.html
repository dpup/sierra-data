Places

Every event and summary in the Grid Info Service is scoped to a place — an area, county, town, evacuation zone, corridor, or site. Places are addressable by slug (ebbetts-pass) or id (county:calaveras-county). This page is the directory, the per-place detail view, and the resolve tester that answers “which places contain this point or address?”

Endpoints

GET /v1/places
Directory. Filters: kind (AREA, COUNTY, TOWN, EVAC_ZONE, CORRIDOR, SITE), q (name search). Try /v1/places?kind=COUNTY.
GET /v1/places/{place}
One place by slug or id, including geometry (base64 GeoJSON in geometry.geojson) and parent_id. Try /v1/places/ebbetts-pass.
GET /v1/places/resolve
?lat=&lng= or ?address= → containing places, most-specific first (SITE → AREA). Try /v1/places/resolve?lat=38.0679&lng=-120.5385.
